One of the big news of the week was that [Ghost will be federating](https://activitypub.ghost.org/?ref=ghost.org). Let's unpack this a bit. Because this is important.

### How it used to work

You would spin up a server, have your website and publish there. You would usually get other people to join you and become a publication.

### Then came the social networks

Well you know, Orkut \(Oh Orkut\!\) and then the big blue Facebook, Instagram, Twitter \(X now\) and so on. 

You log in to a platform. You create your account there. And then you post. But none of it belongs to you. If you don't like where you are, you can't do anything. You are stuck\! Or, you can delete everything you've created and that's that.

That's oversimplifying it a bit. And also there's the issue of the algorithms. But that's a good summary of it.

### Ok, so what is this about?

ActivityPub is a protocol, like HTML for social media. It allows you to be in control of your stuff. It allows people to follow you across platforms. It allows you to leave, if you're unhappy and take your content with you. It is open. 😄

[Threads would someday support this](https://www.theverge.com/2023/12/13/24000120/threads-meta-activitypub-test-mastodon). [They are testing integration with Mastodon](https://www.theverge.com/2024/3/21/24107881/threads-fediverse-beta-launch-mastodon).

### Why am I excited about his?

Because, [this](sajalchoudhary.net) is hosted on Ghost. 

And I would love to make this the centre of my online existence. I would love to create my identity here. An identity I own. An identity I can control. 

I would love to post here once, and have it show at different places.

I would love for this to be the place from where I can interact with people.

Also, this is open\! 😄

## I am thinking about

### How does information flow?

Information and knowledge are different things. 

Information is just information. It's there. Out on a webpage, a quote in a book somewhere.

Information becomes knowledge after it has been processed. After you have digested it, thought about it, added context to it. Then it becomes knowledge, your knowledge.
