They died here that day,  
Died.  
They did not do a noble deed,  
No. They were not martyrs,  
They were victims. Of some general’s desire  
To implement, to enforce, to scare.  
They were not martyrs.  
Had you told them, they’d be killed for gathering in the _bagh_  
They wouldn’t have arrived that day  
I think.  
I wouldn’t have had at least.
