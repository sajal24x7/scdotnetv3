[![The Voice](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/wordpress/2014/03/dsc07387.jpg)](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/wordpress/2014/03/dsc07387.jpg)At the Golden Temple, Amritsar.

Why did we invent language?

To diminish boundaries, or the other way around?

All religion is serene;

It’s we the people, who’ve sort of forgotten

Why we _created_ religion in the first place.

It’s us, always been us,

Who downgraded one religion, or the other;

Ours was always the better one.

We fought wars,

We conquered.
