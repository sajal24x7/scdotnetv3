[The Last of Us Part II’s new mode puts the story in chronological order](https://www.theverge.com/news/701060/the-last-of-us-part-ii-chronological-mode-season-three-hbo)

> [The studio has released a new, free patch for _The Last of Us Part II Remastered_](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/07/08/introducing-the-last-of-us-part-ii-remastered-chronological-experience-out-today/?sf277879873=1) that lets you play the game in chronological order. The mode takes Abby and Ellie’s stories and interleaves them in chronological order rather than the nonlinear story of the original version of the game, which used timeskips and flashbacks.
