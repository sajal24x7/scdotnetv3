[Close Study: Bill Cunningham by John Peabody](https://thisisness.substack.com/p/close-study-bill-cunningham)

> You cannot be creative and inconsistent. You don’t need to be consistently good. You’ll probably be consistently bad for a long stretch, and that’s fine. That’s good actually. That’s the work. What matters is that you keep going. Butts in seats. Fingers on shutters. Hands on strings.
