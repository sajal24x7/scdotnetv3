There’s a new mark above or below as read in NetNewsWire. It feels like it was designed for me. Some feeds that I subscribe to - mostly aggregator style feeds - have too many items in them. I don’t usually have the time to go through all the items in one go. In those cases, I was usually marking all as read without reading or doing nothing. This solves that feature.
