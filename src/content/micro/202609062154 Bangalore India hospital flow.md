[Bangalore India hospital flow by Derek Sivers](https://sive.rs/inflow)

> Medications that cost $50 in New Zealand or $100 in America are only $2 in India. After my surgery, they prescribed a bunch of stuff to keep the pain and infection away. Fulfilled at a local pharmacy, everything all combined cost about $8.
> 
> Need a private consultation with a specialist - like a neurosurgeon or dermatologist or cardiologist? $10. You just tap an appointment in their app, pay instantly with your phone, and that’s it. Which leads to the next point.

We have talked about this ourselves. That healthcare in India is so good and cheap, if you have the money. There are already a lot of people undertaking medical tourism in India. This just strengthens that view.
