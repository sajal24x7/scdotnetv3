[The Safest Job from AI may be Writing](https://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2026/08/the-safest-job-from-ai-may-be-writing.html)

> While the programmer's job description is getting completely refactored, writing remains surprisingly unaffected. LLMs have gotten very good at generating code, but I am appalled at the absolute shit they spew as prose. They always follow the same robotic cadence and cliches, and sprinkle the same tired vocabulary all around. They take my broken yet soulful writing and transform it into a plastic soulless word slop in the name of improving prose. Their writing communicates no actual understanding and insight. I think we are all developing a visceral ick reaction to AI writing. It is trapped in the uncanny valley, and it may be stuck there for a long time.

I remember when writing was considered dead after ChatGPT came out. There were many discussions - blogs, podcasts, and otherwise - declaring writing to be dead. And here we are.

There have been multiple instances now of me recognising a piece of writing as having been AI generated. And that's just sad. Of course LinkedIn is full of AI assisted writing. I guess for people who can't write (or don't feel they can write) it is a great tool. But everything ends up feeling the same.

I had a funny feeling just yesterday when Prerna found a typo in my LinkedIn post. And I thought hey, that's just proof that a human wrote this!
