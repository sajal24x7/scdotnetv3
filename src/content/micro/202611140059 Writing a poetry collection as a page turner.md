[Can You Write a Poetry Collection That’s a Page-Turner? by Kelly Grace Thomas](https://lithub.com/can-you-write-a-poetry-collection-thats-a-page-turner/)

> Because poetry can do this. It can move fast and deep, ask us to feel before we understand, and hold contradiction, mystery, and hard truths at once. And yet poetry has a reputation for being difficult—a slog you have to wade through rather than something you can’t wait to keep reading.
