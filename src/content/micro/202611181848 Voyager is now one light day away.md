[NASA’s Voyager 1 probe is now 1 light-day away by Jess Weatherbed](https://www.theverge.com/science/1036431/nasa-voyager-1-light-day-milestone)

> After traveling through the void of space for 49 years, Voyager 1 has hit a momentous milestone: the spacecraft is now officially one light-day away from Earth, which is, as NASA put it, “very, very far.” The probe crossed the milepost on Wednesday at 5:16AM ET, and is now 16.094 billion miles away from our planet — equal to the distance that light travels in 24 hours.

I saw MKBHD post about it on Threads first and then this post. This is so cool!

I first read it as light year and thought that was cool. I read it now as light day. I find it cooler now. The nearest star to us is four light years away. Light Years.
