Rain,  
like mist.  
Does it pour?  
Or, does it spray?

Does it matter?  
I walked in rain today.  
The ground was wet.  
But different kinds of wet.  
The asphalt had a shine to it.  
The mud, was darker than it would be.

I had put on my cap.  
The spatter of rain,  
Against my ear,  
These were not  
Violent sounds.

It was rain,  
But mild,  
Like mist.

It distorted what I could see.  
It made things muddy.  
Unclear. I took a picture,  
And there was a weird quality to it.  
Not drops of water on the mirror.  
But a foggy, hazy thing.

It was rain.  
But mild,  
like mist.
