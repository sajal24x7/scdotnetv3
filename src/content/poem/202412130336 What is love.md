what is love?

an year has passed since,  
i wondered that out loud last.

a lot has changed,  
over this past year.

an year of firsts,  
it was. is still.

we grew. became more.  
grew fuller perhaps.

we travelled a bunch,  
to places we hadn’t been before.

we tried out new cuisines,  
remember that vietnamese?

we brought life here, in this world.  
now we get to watch it grow.

so much has happened in this year.  
so much has changed.

what has remained the same,  
is how you make me feel.
