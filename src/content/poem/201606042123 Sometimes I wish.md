Sometimes, I wish I were a painter,  
And not a poet,  
So that I had a better reason,  
To sit, and stare,  
To have you, sitting across from me.

You’d be my muse,  
As you are now,  
But not just in my head,  
You’d be out there,  
Physically, in front on me,  
And I’d faint creation.

Sometimes, I wish I were a painter,  
And not a poet.  
Sometimes, I wish I were a painter,  
And not a poet.
