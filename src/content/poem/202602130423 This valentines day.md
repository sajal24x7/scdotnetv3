You said, in Finland,
they celebrate friendships
as well, on this day,
in this week.

It’s love that they celebrate,
regardless of form.

Let us celebrate that,
this valentine’s.
Let us celebrate,
our friendship.

Friendships are different,
from what we share usually.
Friendships are lighter though.
Less burdensome.
Lesser expectations too.
They provide a similar relief though,
a similar sort of love.

Let’s celebrate that too,
this valentine’s.
Being able to talk,
without worrying too much.
Being able to laugh,
at mostly stupid stuff.
Being able to dance,
with or without the music.

Let’s celebrate it all.
Let’s celebrate our friendship,
this valentine’s day.
