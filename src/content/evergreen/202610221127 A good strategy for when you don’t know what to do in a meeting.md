And are expected to drive it.

1. A brief outline of your initial thoughts.
2. Perhaps share your screen and type as you go.

## An example 

As an example, for a migration it may be -
1. Target infrastructure design and cost.
2. Cost of migration 
3. Changes to ways of working because of the migration

These points may change over the duration of the meeting as others chip-in. But it’s the how of the thing that is important. Being collaborative instead of being scared as to what someone may say. Everyone has the same goal - to get to the end state. Though there are some differences based on where they’re coming from.
