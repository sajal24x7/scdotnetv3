For reading books, primarily.

---

It’s a good idea to complete one chapter each day. I have read a few chapters right now from the book [Inside the Box](/bookshelf/inside-the-box). Of course this is not meant to dissuade oneself from reading. Like one should not think that they need enough time to complete a chapter, otherwise don’t start. Rather, it’s take some time and complete the chapter you start.

Why? I guess because chapters are structured that way for the benefit of the reader. A chapter is supposed to contain a semi-independent thing which can exist on its own. 

---

Also, after reading so many chapters, I felt like taking a break. These things are important, even though I could continue reading, I mean I have time. But it feels too much like an information overload - a reason why I don’t want to do audiobooks for non-narrative non-fiction books. Also it’s difficult to pause the audiobook and take notes.
