[OpenAI’s next big launch could be an AI web browser](https://www.theverge.com/news/704162/opeani-ai-web-browser-chatgpt)

> OpenAI is planning to launch an AI web browser in the “coming weeks,” according to [a report from _Reuters_](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/openai-release-web-browser-challenge-google-chrome-2025-07-09/). Sources tell the outlet that OpenAI could build [its Operator AI agent](https://www.theverge.com/2025/1/23/24350395/openai-chatgpt-operator-agent-control-computer) into the browser, allowing it to book reservations, fill out forms, and complete other tasks on a user’s behalf as it moves toward [an “agentic” future](https://www.theverge.com/2024/10/10/24266333/ai-agents-assistants-openai-google-deepmind-bots).

Quite a few companies are working on building AI browsers - Perplexity, OpenAI and The Browser Company. I have not tried any by now. 

All these companies seem to have the same vision - they will use the browser for us, book stuff, search stuff, etc. I don’t know how I feel about that, tbh.
