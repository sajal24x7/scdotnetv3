Like Covid, or any of the great depressions. Sure in hindsight it seems obvious, but for people living the happening, it’s anything but.

There are two things to do then:
1. Plan for risk without prediction. You will not when it comes but you must plan anyway.
2. Always plan more than what might seem ok - like in personal finance it should feel like you have saved up more than needed for the emergency fund.
