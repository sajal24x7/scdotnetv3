# The Ranchos and the Silencers

So, the results came out a while back.

Though, there was a bit of surprise involved yet it might prove to be the best thing to have happened in the past four semesters. It was bad. But then, it got me thinking, thinking about the _Rancho_ s and the _Silencer_ s.

The _Silencer_ s, well, they exist and that too in numbers. They get the grades, should be getting top-notch jobs. The same group might then go on to buy the _Lamborghini_ s and what not\! But what about the _Rancho_ s?

3 Idiots was a nice movie, but well, in the end it was just that; a movie: fiction. I don’t think _Rancho_ s can survive in this great Education system of ours. The novel in that matter, was closer to reality.

Ah\! So long from writing, and this is my first blog post\! 😀

Life’s funny\!
