## five things to share

#### 1\. Suggestions to improve your life

[I came across this Reddit thread about life hacks.](https://www.reddit.com/r/AskReddit/comments/1ff2r5g/whats_your_i_cant_believe_other_people_dont_do/?utm_source=substack&utm_medium=email) Some of the highlights for me:

  1. I have a keyboard shortcut on all my devices where @@ automatically enters my email address. On iOS go to: Settings>General>Keyboard>Text Replacement
  2. I am shocked at the number of people that don’t put their sheet sets in the matching pillow case for storage until use. It keeps it all together\!
  3. Do I need or do I want. If I need it, I get it. If I want it, I wait for a few weeks, do some research, see if I can fit it in and if I still want it a month or two later then I might go get it. Turns out my wants are not always there after a while waiting. It’s new for me but has changed my mindset drastically.
