I had dinner at [Shinobi](https://www.shinobi.fi) on Thursday night. This is a Japanese restaurant near Kamppi in Helsinki. The restaurant has two sections: 

  1. Shokudo - the eat and drink section 
  2. Izakaya - the bar and snacks section

We had booked a table in Shokudo section. The restaurant has a unique sharing menu, wherein every item was supposed to be shared between two members.

The [menu](https://www.shinobi.fi/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Shokudo-menu-web-1.pdf) was not structured in a three-course meal fashion. Instead we started with sashimi and spicy edamame, followed by scallops and this wonderful crispy rice topped with avocado. The tofu was the best tofu I've ever had. I don't like tofu. It tastes weird, paneer is usually better. But man this tofu was so good.

For desserts, half the table got the chocolate cake, and the other half Lemon cake. I was in the lemon cake half. It was great. Everything was great here. The food, the service, the ambiance.

I will definitely visit again.
