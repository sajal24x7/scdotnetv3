I happened upon Austin Kleon’s [website](https://austinkleon.com) somehow. I have known him from reading [Steal like an author](https://austinkleon.com/steal/). Coincidentally I found out that he blogs often. Often daily.  
This is something that I have struggled with often. And I keep coming back to it again and again. Should I write daily, or weekly? Should I post something even if I am not happy with it?  
Insight is the key thing. If I am not adding anything to something, then you can go to the verge and read it there I guess. Also, quality and quantity both are important. Why does it have to be one or the other? Why can't it be both?  
Anyway, that's a roundabout way of saying I will be writing more often.  
Writing is not just done for the sake of writing, for the sake of publishing. When we write, we clarify our thought. We give structure to it.

> I write to think.

There's no harm in doing more of it. Right?

## /techStuff

  1. Apple announced new iPhones, Airpods, and Apple Watches on Monday. [I wrote about it here](__GHOST_URL__/iphone-day/)
     1. To summarise, the event felt twice as long as it should have been.
     2. There was not actually that much new stuff announced.
     3. The Airpods 4 have ANC option.
     4. The Airpods 2 can work as over the counter hearing-aids.
     5. The Apple Watches are larger now\!
     6. There is a camera control button on the iPhones now.
     7. The iPhones are iterative updates but those iterations become substantive over multiple generations. I ordered the 256GB iPhone 16 Pro. I will be upgrading from the 13Pro so I am very excited.
  2. [Sony launched the PS5 Pro](https://www.theverge.com/2024/9/11/24241706/sony-ps5-pro-price-next-gen-consoles). At 700USD it is a costly purchased. I do not see why one would upgrade from the base PS5, but if you are getting a new PS5 today, sure a good option to have along with the slim.
  3. [Huawei announced/launched a tri-fold phone](https://www.theverge.com/2024/9/10/24240700/huawei-mate-xt-ultimate-design-tri-fold-price-launch). Yes, tri-fold. It costs as much as a 16-inc Macbook Pro. I guess this is the week of costly device announcements?
