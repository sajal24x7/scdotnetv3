There is an inevitability to life. We are born. We will die one day. There is a genetic component to many things we do, or are.

But who we are, is what we do.

Between the inevitabilities of who we are supposed to be, we make decisions, we act, we do things which define us.

It is these things that people talk about or miss after a person dies. It is these things that define who you are.
