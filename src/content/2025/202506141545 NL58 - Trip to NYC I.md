## /trip to NYC

We visited NYC in May. We stayed in Midtown Manhattan for the eight nights we were in NYC. Before flying we were a bit worried about immigration with all the Trump news going on. We did not face any issues during immigration.

### day 1
We left Helsinki at 5PM and reached JFK around 7PM. Hello jet-lag!
The JFK airport is not pretty. It feels like a Tier-2 city airport in India. That is the thought I had while waiting in the immigration line. It got dark by the time we left the airport. The city did not feel impressive then. There was a ton of construction happening around the airport. And, it was dark. 

Then, we saw the Manhattan skyline from across the bridge. There truly is no place like New York.

![Manhattan skyline at night](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/493DADD8-FFB4-4E3B-83B5-7AB1E6B48F4B_1_102_o.jpeg)
### day 2

Did I say already - there is no place like NYC. 

![Manhattan skyline in the morning](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/071A0B91-FEE9-4E55-9C7C-13944050C2C2_1_102_o.jpeg)

For the next couple of days we walked around with a sprain in our necks from constantly looking up while walking. It finally made sense to me why Spider Man had to have happened here.

We ate our breakfast at Saravana Bhavan, which was a cool 2.5 kms from where we were staying. From there, we walked to Central Park. On our way there we saw the Chrysler building and Grand Central Station.

Our friends joined us in Central Park. Central Park is huge, an oasis of nature in this bustling metropolis. We walked its trails, stood on a meadow overlooking the massive skyline, saw the Bethesda fountain, the carousel, and then exited from the American Natural History museum side.

![Central Park](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/750E4666-D7EF-49F0-9935-066B94B1861A 1.jpg)

![Central Park](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/D12127E0-1FA8-4683-AF65-6E0E7ADB41FD_1_102_o.jpeg)

We ate our lunch at a Korean restaurant. It was an adventure in more ways than one.

Then, we roamed around town. We saw the Louis Vuitton building, the Trump Tower, the Rockefeller Center. Finally, we walked to my mecca, the 5th Avenue Apple Store. 

![Apple Store](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/266AD730-3B25-40E3-B89D-DBA4C9032082_1_105_c.jpeg)

This was my first time in any Apple store. And this, well this is, **the**
Apple store. The store is massive. It might not look like it from the outside but most of it is a level lower. There is a glass elevator, with no visible wires anywhere. There are tables upon table of iPhones, Macs and iPads, walls upon walls of accessories and Apple TVs, and a veritable army of Apple Geniuses. 

![Inside the Apple Store](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/432C9B13-3F23-4DD4-9E66-ADCAD29509FA_1_105_c.jpeg)

![Glass elevator](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/D3B040F9-04ED-4105-B981-CDFE865D822A_1_105_c.jpeg)

I *had* to buy something while I was here. And so I did. A steel band for my Apple watch. Over two further visits I got an iPad Air and a Macbook Air as well. Prerna got a band for her watch as well.

We ended the day with a banana pudding from Magnolia Bakery.

### day 3

We started the day with the breakfast at Saravana Bhavan.

I saw the first of these sorts of wine/beer/coffee/tea + books places. NYC has a bunch of these. I love this.

![Book store](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/8891C3B8-7C51-4068-A1E0-754DB75D503A_1_105_c.jpeg)

We took the ferry from Pier 11 to DUMBO. It's a short ferry ride, but what it allows you to walk the Brooklyn Bridge from the Brooklyn side.

![At the Brooklyn pier](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/1C858CC4-002F-4007-AD5F-ED0DF0E62AA3_1_102_o.jpeg)

Savya enjoyed himself at the pier and the Brooklyn Bridge park. For some reason, he was scared of the grass though. We walked to Dumbo, and got a picture taken there. It was full of tourists though. 

![DUMBO](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/74AA0210-5D22-43D2-B033-0F6CB1E02190_1_102_o.jpeg)

From there, we walked to Juliana's pizza. The pizza was great, so fresh. I saw a gentleman behind me order an ice cream and an espresso, pour the espresso a little bit at a time over the ice-cream and eat it. I had to have that. And so I did.

![Julianna's Pizza](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/2D377DE3-488D-4234-80F5-C6934E2A0CDA_1_102_o.jpeg)

![Pizza](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/659C6E60-0350-4AC6-B648-B868C2DAA0A3_1_102_o.jpeg)

![Sweet dish](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/2F4C2AE6-8435-4A82-AB2D-1555A01398E6_4_5005_c.jpeg)

The reason you take the ferry from Pier11 to DUMBO, is so that while walking the Brooklyn Bridge the Manhattan skyline is in front of you. 

![On the Brooklyn Bridge](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/FE155C26-FF5C-4867-A50A-536F38CF7205_1_102_o.jpeg)

We ended the day with a jaunt through South Manhattan - the 9/11 memorial, the World Trade Center Oculus, the NYSE. I got a hot dog from a stand. It was not good. The picture is nice though.

![Hot dog with NYSE blurred](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/06/EC6CB073-10F7-4043-A483-09B542B2AC95_1_102_o.jpeg)

We ended Day 3 with a dinner at Spice Symphony, an Indian restaurant which had both delicious food and was across the street from where we were staying.

The rest of the days will follow in the next NordLetter.
