# How to Run Your Enterprise Cloud

![First talk](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/10/aaf-oct-3.jpeg)

Ilkka Kaartinen gave pointers on running enterprise cloud.

He talked about the holy trinity of cloud:
1. Awareness - what do you have - also skills you have 
2. Control - manage what you have
3. Cost - understand the different forms of cost

Use centralized governance

## How to setup
- have a center of excellence
	- needs to have a executive sponsor

- Requires -
1. Strong domain owners 
2. Up to date CMDB
3. No siloes
4. Open minded lead devs

# Subscription Expiry Alerts

![Second talk](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2025/10/aaf-oct-4.jpeg)

Next up Sureshbabu Rajasekaran took us through a Logic App workflow which would alert the CCoE on subscription expiry and disable those automatically upon expiry.

1. Tagging is at the core 
	1. Tags provide context, enable automation and support reporting 
	2. Ensure owner tag has email
	3. Audit tags regularly 
2. Why?
	1. To save costs
	2. Governance
3. How?
	1. Logic app workflow
