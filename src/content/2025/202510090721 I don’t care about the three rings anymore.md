I used to be obsessed with the Apple Health and workout metric. 

I would make sure it would be listed on the Apple Watch - I used to have the workout watch face as my watch face so that I had easy access to it, all the time. Now my watch face is the photos one. I guess one reason for losing all these widgets and these data rich watch faces is the new interaction where scrolling on the dial brings up the widget.

On my iPhone I would add it as a widget on the Home Screen or lock screen. Again, glancibility was key. 

I thought of this today while changing my lock screen widgets. I picked up my phone and wanted to be able to see the weather info and it wasn’t there. So I decided to set it.

One of the options was workout and I remembered the old me.
