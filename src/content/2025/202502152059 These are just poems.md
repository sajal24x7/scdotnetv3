# These are just poems

These are just poems,  
And not serious ones at that.

There are not going to  
start revolutions and win wars\!

These are just poems\!  
You read them, nestled in  
the cozy corner in your home,  
your bed or your sofa.

You read them,  
with a hot cup of tea in your hand,  
while the world is white,  
with fresh snow all around.

These are just poems\!  
You read them and smile,  
or laugh, or cry. Whatever.  
You do something.

And that is all\!  
These are just poems after all\!
