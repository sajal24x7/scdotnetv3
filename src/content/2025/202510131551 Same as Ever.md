I loved the book. Like [the psychology of money](#), this book is structured as a series of short essays on very slightly connected topics.

The thesis of this book is that there are a bunch of things that do not change through time. We should know what these things are, and try to live our life based on this knowledge.

I made a bunch of notes thanks to this book - so it was a great hit by that measurement.

A few things were a little surprising, but this book, like the [Devops Finland meetup](/blog/devops-finland-october-meetup)  I was in yesterday, made me realise the value of knowing and linking diverse things. Reading more. Writing more.
