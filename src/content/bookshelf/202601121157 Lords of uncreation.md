There is a bitter-sweet feeling I have now as I have finished this book. 

You feel that way when you finish a series. Till this point of course I knew I could spend more time with Solace, Idris, Ollie, Chris and the others. Not any more.

I loved it. 

The great end of the architects and their masters. There was only one way Idris could have ended. And I had that feeling since perhaps the middle of the book.

Solace was my favourite character. Ollie was fun. The whole she inheriting the title of the book and the razer was so awesome. 

Such fun characters. Such great universe.
