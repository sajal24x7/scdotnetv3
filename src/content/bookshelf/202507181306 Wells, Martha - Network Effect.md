This is the fifth book in Martha Wells' [The Murderbot Diaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Murderbot_Diaries) series, and the first full length novel.

Given my record with this series, this should have been the not-so-great book, but it was.

The thing that I had loved about the series so far was that the books were small length, like [A Psalm for the Wild-Built](#). I guess I prefer novella length books, given the time I have now.
