This is the first book in the Murderbot series. And a nice change of pace from [Death's End by Cixin Liu](https://sajalchoudhary.net/bookshelf/cixin-liu-the-dark-forest/) which was the last fiction book I read.

It is short.
It is funny. 

I have discovered audio books recently. This is the second book I’ve read by hearing it on Audible. The first was [Abundance by Ezra Klein and Derek Thompson](https://sajalchoudhary.net/bookshelf/abundance-how-we-build-a-better-future/). And in my very limited experience with audiobooks, I feel fiction is better suited to the format. With non-fiction, I tend to take notes and that is hard with audiobooks.
