This is a very short book with some wonderful ideas or advices. I finished it in a day.

Some quotes from the book.

> Expand your mind by thinking with your feet on a walk or with your hand in a notebook. Think outside your brain.

> Be more generous than necessary. No one on their deathbed has ever  
> regretted giving too much away. There is no point to being  
> the richest person in the cemetery.

> Everyone’s time is finite and shrinking. The highest leverage you can get with your money is to buy someone else’s time. Hire and outsource when you can.
