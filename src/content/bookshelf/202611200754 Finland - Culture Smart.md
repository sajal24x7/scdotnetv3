## Review

This book was available to borrow and I was in-between books. Plus Prerna tends to ask me questions from time-to-time about Finland which I don't have answers to. So I thought why not.

This books exists as something you would read when travelling to Finland. I realised that there's value in that. And I felt I should have done it before travelling to Barcelona. It may be something I do before travelling to a new place from now on. It would improve and enhance the trip. We do that now by looking at Youtube videos. But books tend to go deeper, as did this one.

This book serves as a good map to who Finns are and what Finland is as a country. Most of the these things I knew about already, having lived here for the past five years. But those are acquired things, things that someone asked at a time, or I needed to know, and so I googled and they got added to my memory.

There were still things that were new to me. Which I noted down below. Like the thing about flags was something that Prerna and I discussed recently.

I recommend this book if you are curious about Finland and its culture.
## Notes

Barley and Oats are the major crops grown in Finland.

All Finnish National Holidays are flag days, though there are other days which are also flag days like Mother’s Day.

Turning 18 and 50 are celebrated in Finland. People celebrate birthdays and name-days.
