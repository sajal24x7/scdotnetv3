So many things to take as lessons from this book, the third and final Kleon book on creative work.

This one has advice for how to keep doing the work - in good times and bad. There are a ton of good advices, from making lists to going on walks, from making gifts to playing like children.

This will be another book that I will re-read time and again. 

## Quotes

> It is easy to assume that if only you could trade your ordinary life for a new one, all your creative problems would be solved. If only you could quit your day job, move to a hip city, rent the perfect studio, and fall in with the right gang of brilliant misfits! Then you'd really have it made.
> 
> All this is, of course, wishful thinking. You do not need to have an extraordinary life to make extraordinary work.
> 
> Everything you need to make extraordinary art can be found in your everyday lite.
