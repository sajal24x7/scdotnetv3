## Review

Picked this up after finishing Harlan Coben's [Plot Twist](/bookshelf/plot-twist). 

The core idea of the book is not one but a few things - what if you really loved a person and they were taken from you, and you could not move on, even after eight damn years. And then, what if after those eight years you get a email with a link to a camera, where you see that person, alive and well.

I was looking to read a thriller for quite some time now. I'm usually reading sci-fi and the setting in sci-fis is a major part of the story. Here, not so much. So then, as a writer you can put in twists and turns and take the story to new places. Which Harlan did, and how.

I recommend it. Harlan also has a long running spy-series. Maybe I will pick that up next. Or a different book like I will find you, perhaps?
