## Review

I was in [Barcelona](/nordletter/nl118-trip-to-barcelona) when I first heard my friend talk about Harlan Coben and his thrillers, and how she loved watching his shows. I had seen [I will find you](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Will_Find_You) show up in Netflix, and was curious about it. All that too say I was mildly aware of Harlan, before going to Barcelona, but di not know he was a super successful writer. Then, a few weeks back I heard him on the [How I write podcast](https://howiwrite.substack.com/p/harlan-coben-tough-love-advice-for), searched for the book in the library and then read it when it became available.

This book is part memoir, part writing advice in the vain of Stephen King's On writing and [Bird by bird](/bookshelf/bird-by-bird) by Anne Lamott. Maybe 80% writing and 20% memoir. There are plenty of stories all through the book, but primarily, this book is about how to write.

The lessons are all practical. The book as a whole is motivating. And it stays with you - at least it has with me. 

Read it, if you like books on craft.
