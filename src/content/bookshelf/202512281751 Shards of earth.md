I loved Shards of Earth.

This is the first book in the final architecture series. I picked it up over the expanse series. 

This is a space opera of gigantic proportions. There are a ton of mysteries still left, but most of the promises Adrian made were paid off by the end of the book.

I love the Parthenon. I loved the characters - solace the most. I loved the book so much so that even though I had bought it on Audible I continued reading it over anything else.

I am looking forward to the next book.
