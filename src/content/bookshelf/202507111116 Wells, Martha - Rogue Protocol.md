This is the third book in Martha Wells' [The Murderbot Diaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Murderbot_Diaries) series.

Murderbot is back! Yearning for his conversations with ART from [Artificial Condition](https://sajalchoudhary.net/bookshelf/wells-martha-artificial-condition/), alas what he has to contend with here is a dumb transport bot, and a toy bot. There is a nice satisfying arc and end to that relationship though.

There are more dumb humans to save. More villainous plots to be thwarted. All in all an average day in the office for our beloved murderbot.

I did not like this one as much as [Artificial Condition](https://sajalchoudhary.net/bookshelf/wells-martha-artificial-condition/). I liked it better than [All systems red](https://sajalchoudhary.net/bookshelf/wells-martha-all-systems-red/). The first half of the book was OK. The next was better after the characters were all in place.

I am looking forward to the next book in the series, given my history with the series. 

1,3 - OK. 
2 - Great.
