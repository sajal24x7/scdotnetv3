## Review

The eight book in the series, and like the others an entertaining read - I laughed out loud while walking and doing other things in the public. I don’t do that usually.

Murderbot is on an extraction mission in this one, continuing on from [[202603101909 System Collapse|System Collapse]]. I enjoyed almost everything about the book. I enjoyed the fight sequences and Murderbot trying to just watch his damn show.

Recommended, if you are already invested in the series.
