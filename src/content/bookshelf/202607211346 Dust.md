## Review

The finale to the Silo series (Hugh did announce the fourth book in the series coming out next year perhaps).

We follow Juliette, Donny and the gang as they barrel toward a confrontation. It was due today, and so I pushed through finishing it today. I was engrossed in the book too. And when I get like that I tend to finish the book quickly. And so both the things happened here.
