## Review

This book has been on my to read list for a long time, ever since I saw [Becoming Warren Buffett](https://m.imdb.com/title/tt6438096/) and saw Mr. Buffett recommending this book.

I had purchased this book on Kindle, but could never get to it. I could finish it this time perhaps because I had reserved it at the library, though in all fairness, I think I will have to re-read it, in order to make notes and get something useful out of it.

The book is technical, and goes over the same principles I believe in and follow - which perhaps comes from people who have read this book and follow it. So, there was not much newness in this book for me.

I would recommend you read it though.
