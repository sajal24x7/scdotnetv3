This movie showed up as #1 on Netflix and I was looking for something to watch. So I watched it.

It is a good movie.

I have been thinking about gender roles and politics thanks to a book talk I gave recently about Children of Time.

This movie takes the view that if roles were reversed women will behave the way men do and then runs with it.

That’s a less interesting approach I think, but philosophy aside, I enjoyed it. It is fun.
