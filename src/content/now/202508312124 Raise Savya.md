Savya is my child. He is going to päiväkoti now. Raising him continues to be the best thing in my life.

---

2026-08-10 12:17
Savya calls all animals dogs. 
After the Barcelona trip (or rather during the trip ), he has started to do two new things - asking to get his photos clicked and asking for rhymes to be sung to him when he goes to sleep. When he was smaller I had tried the Nindiya Re song with him, it hadn’t really stuck earlier. Now it has. Now he says, “haule haule”, when he wants me to sing it.
