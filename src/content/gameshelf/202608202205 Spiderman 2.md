Finished it today. I loved the ending of the game. So many people including me love Spider-Man because his struggles are normal people struggles, and the final scene felt like a nice nod to that.

I have not really enjoyed the grind of the game. The fights are just button mashing, and so I was in a hurry to finish the story. I did not enjoy any of the side quests. Like all of them are basically extensions of the same thing spread throughout the map. It feels like you are doing the same thing again and again. Unlike [Ghost of Yotei](/gameshelf/ghost-of-yotei) say, where the combat is satisfying, and it has a story to it. I prefer that.

Another reason I rushed through it was because GTA VI comes out soon, and I wanted to wipe the slate clean so to say.

I liked it, but not as much as I had enjoyed the first one.
