What a day, today!
I saw a black box, at a distance,
As I started out for my walk today.
The box stirred, 
I got scared. 
It turned into a rabbit,
Jumped up out of the way.

It rains. Harder now.
Bigger drops. 
The water in the bay,
Has never been this high.
Parts of the road,
Are under water.
And just as I walk away 
from the gurgling water,
I come across another box - a fox!
The kind I find, in the digital 
grasslands of Tsushima.
It scampers away, 
Before I go to pet it anyway.

What a day, today!
