What do you do when you see your reflection;  
Not in the water, or a mirror either. But  
In a person.  
Its almost like seeing a ghost.  
What do you do then?  
Reach out?  
Try to grab it?  
Talk to it?  
Or do you scamper off in your closet? Scared.
