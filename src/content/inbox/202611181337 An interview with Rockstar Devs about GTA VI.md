[‘It’s a risk’: How Rockstar built the most detailed (and romantic) GTA yet by Andrew Webster](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/1035806/grand-theft-auto-6-rob-nelson-interview)

> While RDR2 was a widely praised game, it also received criticism for its slow pace, which was due in large part to all of the small mechanical details. These added animations and button prompts that gave texture to the world but could also lead to a plodding pace. That would be a much bigger problem in a game like GTA, which is built around action. Nelson says that he can be “freakishly obsessive” about these kinds of details, but he and the team had to learn to let go when realism got in the way of the game itself.
