## Review

1929 tells the story of the great market crash and the resultant depression in America.

I like history, not because of the facts of the thing, but because of the lesson that it holds. Too often, we don't care about the lessons, to our own detriment. These things tend to keep repeating after all. 

The lesson here, from all the stories was that none of these people felt like they were making any mistakes. Especially in the epilogue there was a story about one of the bankers helping out his relative with money - his relative was a crook. Goes to show, so many of these things happening on people’s words, who they were and so on.

It was an interesting book. I recommend it.
