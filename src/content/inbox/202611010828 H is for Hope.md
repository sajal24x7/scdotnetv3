## Review
This is a short book -  collection of 26 essays - the A to Z of climate change.

Even though its title has hope in its name, thanks to humanity, and the topic of climate change, the essays left me feeling anxious, instead of hopeful. There were maybe two or three essays that left me hopeful for the future - about electrification, electric planes and so on.

We have been behaving as if someone would solve this problem for us, with regard to climate change. No one would. We need to fix it ourselves. 

There was another good point here - that we need to have good images of the future to be motivated to work toward it. Something good and hopeful.
