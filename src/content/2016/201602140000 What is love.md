# What is love?

Love is that thing which wakes you up, in the middle of the night, only to look at the other sleeping in peace, and wonder, how beautiful they are\!

Love is that thing which makes you go the extra mile for them, even when you are broken, tired; to do the thing that will make them happy.

Love is the thing we may never find. Love is the thing that can not be seen. Love is the air that surrounds us. Love is the chain, that frees us.
