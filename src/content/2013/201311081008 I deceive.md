**I deceive people with words all day,**

I lie,

I tell them to live, to die one day\!

I deceive people with words all day\!

**Who am I?**

What am I?

A bin? A dump?

Good? Bad?

Am I God?

**Mind is all that matters,**

Mind is what drives, decides, brings you down\!

Mind is the soul, the heart of man\!

Mind is the friend, the enemy of man\!

Mind is growth, mind is failure.

Mind is birth, death, destruction.

**Who am I? What am I?**

Am I God?

For I teach control;

Or a mere man?

For I help, I guide.

**I deceive people with words  all day\!**

I lie.

I tell them to live to die one day\!

I give them hope, assurances.

Life is beautiful,

People are beautiful.

They live, to die someday.

I live, broken, beaten, scarred

I loose myself in them each day

I live to lie, to deceive, to die one day\!
