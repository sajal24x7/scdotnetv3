I saw your bindi,  
on the mirror in our room.  
I saw your bindi,  
stuck on a wall in our shower.  
And I thought,  
Prerna was here.  
This is a lived in place.  
These rooms, this home.

It felt as if you were talking to me,  
Saying hi, from a different time.
