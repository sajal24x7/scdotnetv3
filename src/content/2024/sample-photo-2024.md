# Milky Way Over the Mountains

Captured this breathtaking view of the Milky Way during a clear night in the mountains. The long exposure reveals the intricate details of our galaxy's core and the surrounding star fields.

## Technical Details

- Camera: Sony A7III
- Lens: 24mm f/1.4
- Exposure: 20 seconds
- ISO: 3200
- Location: Mountain Observatory
- Date: April 15, 2024
