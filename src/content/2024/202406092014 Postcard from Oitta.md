Hello from my home in Matinkylä\! This is _NordLetter_ \#11, a weekly newsletter on living and walking in Finland. Previous editions can be found [here](__GHOST_URL__/nordletter/).

* * *

We visited [Oitta beach](https://www.espoo.fi/en/units/39322) this Saturday. [BJPRF](https://www.instagram.com/bjpf_ry?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet&igsh=ZDNlZDc0MzIxNw==) had organised a picnic at the beach.

Oitta beach is located on the shores of Lake Bodom. From Matinkylä we took the 520 till [Lommila](__GHOST_URL__/nordletter/a-trip-to-ikea/) and then 246 to Oitta. It was a short 5 min walk from the bus stop to the beach.

Here's the parking space that greets you on the way.

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3593.jpeg)Parking space

And the angry bird park, also on the way.

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3597.jpeg)

And finally, the beach.

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3632.jpeg)Beach+Swimming pools

The beach is suitable for families, as the water deepens gradually. There were separate _pools_ with varying depths created by the walkway. 

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3602.jpeg)

The water like any where else in Finland was clean and pristine.

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3651.jpeg)

We had delicious Indian Food \(kachori + aloo sabzi + dal makhani + pulao + gulab jamun\).

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_8624.jpeg)

We played games.

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_8630.jpeg)

And we got our pictures clicked.

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3631.jpeg)

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3618.jpeg)

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3663.jpeg)

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3650.jpeg)

There is also a manor/barn there, which is now a recreation centre. But we did not use it. It looked closed. Maybe because of the weather as it was supposed to be a rainy weekend. And it was in spots\! But mostly it was sunny.

![](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2024/06/IMG_3654.jpeg)

To be fair, I had not expected much out of this trip. But Oitta is a beautiful place. The beach is clean and beautiful and there is plenty to do. It was a bit nippy so we did not swim. Next time we will.
