# Understanding Space-Time Continuum

The space-time continuum is one of the most fascinating concepts in modern physics. This article explores the fundamental principles that govern our understanding of space and time.

## The Basics

Space-time is a mathematical model that combines space and time into a single continuum. This concept was first introduced by Albert Einstein in his theory of relativity.

## Key Concepts

1. Four-dimensional space
2. Time dilation
3. Space-time curvature
4. Gravitational effects

## Practical Applications

Understanding space-time is crucial for:
- GPS navigation
- Space travel
- Quantum computing
- Modern cosmology
