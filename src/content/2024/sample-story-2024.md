# The Last Transmission

The signal came in the middle of the night. A repeating pattern of pulses that defied all known natural phenomena. Dr. Sarah Chen was the first to notice it, her bleary eyes widening as the data scrolled across her screen.

## The Discovery

It wasn't the first time they'd received unusual signals from deep space, but this one was different. The pattern was too precise, too deliberate to be natural. And it was getting stronger.

## The Investigation

The team worked around the clock, analyzing every aspect of the transmission. The source was moving, that much was clear. But where was it going? And more importantly, who was sending it?

## The Revelation

As the days passed, the signal began to change. It wasn't just a pattern anymore—it was a message. And it was meant for them.
