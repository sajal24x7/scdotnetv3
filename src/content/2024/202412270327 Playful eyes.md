Playful eyes.  
Looking here,  
Looking there,  
And now back here.

You are looking  
for things to get  
your hands on,  
things you could  
put in your mouth.

Why?  
Does the TV remote taste  
absolutely delicious?  
Should I taste it?

Do you need to smack  
some sense into the actress  
on that show?  
Should I try it?  
Will it stop her  
from going into that basement?

Playful eyes.  
Looking for things to grab.  
Playful eyes.  
When you look at me,  
it feels as if you are  
peering into my soul.

Soulful eyes.  
Dreamy eyes.  
Playful eyes.
