# Cosmic Dance

In the vast theater of space,
Stars pirouette in endless grace,
Planets waltz in perfect time,
To a rhythm so sublime.

Galaxies swirl in cosmic streams,
Like dancers lost in distant dreams,
While comets streak across the night,
In flashes of celestial light.

The universe, a grand ballet,
Performed each night and every day,
A dance that's older than the sun,
And will continue when we're done.
