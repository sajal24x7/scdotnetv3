# Quick Thought: The Scale of the Universe

Looking up at the night sky tonight, I'm reminded that we're just a tiny speck in an unimaginably vast universe. Each star we see is a sun, potentially with its own planets, and we're just one of billions of galaxies.

The scale is both humbling and inspiring.
