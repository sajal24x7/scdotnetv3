# Year One

This here, is year one. A year to the day we got married.

I love you Prerna.

> I love your positivity, your smile,   
> the mischief in your eye\!  
> I love your lips, your smile,  
> the litheness in your hips.

We have done so much this year. And yet, it feels like it was only yesterday that we sat around the holy fire, and took our vows.

That's relativity in action\! When you're having so much fun that you don't know where the time went. 

A full year\!
