[Mark announced changes to way it does content moderation across Meta properties - IG, FB, Threads.](https://www.instagram.com/p/DEhf2uTJUs0/)

The main announcement was a video from Zuckerberg, and then a [thread on Threads](https://www.threads.net/@zuck/post/DEhgYx4JbEG).

There were [a lot of takes on this](https://www.wired.com/story/plaintext-meta-zuckerberg-maga-trump/). Mostly everyone agreeing that Zuckerberg has catapulted to Trump and made worse changes. I think, as do many others, that Meta never had any spine. The whole Meta loves Fediverse thing was a sham. And God bless America\!

The noise on Threads was so much that I deleted it for the time being. It might remain

I liked [John's coverage of this announcement at Daring Fireball,](https://daringfireball.net/2025/01/meta_zuck_content_moderation_zig_zag) the best. It was concise, to the point and not screaming, the world is ending. John went over all the points in Mark's announcement.

I agree with him on most points, especially the fact that the problem is not who is allowed to say what, the problem is amplification. If all Meta did was show a chronological feed, then great. But they do not. Meta wants engagement, above all.

> The question isn’t what sort of posts Meta is now going to allow, but rather, what sort of posts are their algorithms going to promote, and to whom.

I highly recommend you read it. If nothing else.

* * *

I have removed Threads from my phone. This is not a political statement.

  1. I caught myself spending more time on Threads. Idle time. So I removed it. I do this with all social media apps. In the past, I have done this with IG, Reddit, LinkedIn.
  2. I enjoy Mastodon more now. I did not in the beginning, but it has grown on me. I like the crowd here. The timeline is chronological. If I want to see things from elsewhere, I can go to a tab and look at popular things. What an idea, eh Mark?

* * *

It will be interesting to see how things evolve in this space. Will we get an open social web?
