Positivity can be contagious. When dealing with others, people can pick up on positivity. They might think you're weird. Because positivity is so scarce in this world.

You need to be this person, in order to receive the positivity in the world.

I am not that person. Usually, I have my headphones on, listening to something, reading a book or some article on my phone.

My first thought, reaction to something asked of me, is usually, no.

No, I don't to go to the beach.

No, I don't want to go to the mall.

No, I don't think this super complex thing can be done in a week.

Even if you feel something will be close. There might be a 50-50 chance if something will be done successfully. It would be beneficial to be positive. It might be the thing that helps us succeed. It will lift those around us for sure.

Be positive.
