I am travelling to India today. 

Prerna asked how was I feeling as I was boarding the P train to the airport. 

I feel stressed I said. Even though I was on time. Early is on time. 

Travelling on planes is not seamless. You have to prepare for boarding a flight, go through security, wait for boarding to begin.

It is not as seamless as boarding a bus or a train. Even though the airport here at Helsinki is the most seamless one I have been to.

As I type this, I am sitting at 65A. I don’t know who will sit next to me. The seat is empty at present. I will be very surprised if it remains that way.

Now back to the book.
