He’s gone; the Lord  
He has left.  
He’s in the mountains, alone.  
He’s not looking down, nor up.  
Not right or left.  
He’s looking inside. Looking.  
He’s smiling.  
He’s sad, and angry. But not happy.  
He’s smiling, the Lord.  
He has left.  
He’s in the mountains.
