## Meta-Harnessing by Joonas Kykkänen, Wunderdog

![Harness Engineering Talk](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2026/11/vibe-nov-02.jpeg)*Harness Engineering Talk*

Researchers found that context files increase cost but don’t improve task success rates. So we should measure it. 

The idea is that a improver agent looks for ways to improve the harness/instructions. He performed two experiments with meta-harness and harness^2. With the disclaimer that the experiment does not prove anything, so no point in talking about the results.

With meta-harness, you need a large list of tasks that it could train on, so that part is expensive. The learning is cheap then, that means we can do it in our company and then ship it to user, so user does not see it.

Harness^2 on the other hand does not need a great expensive dataset. But the learning is expensive, so end user may care more about it.
## Harnessing the World Around You by Jarno Silander, Founder of Kinoon fi and Djust

![Harness everything around you](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2026/11/vibe-nov-03.jpeg)*Harness everything around you Talk*

He built a platform (djust) for making a DJ’s life easier, removing the admin tasks and letting the artist focus on the art.

The idea is that you can use all the devices around you and use them to make your life better. Provide your agents some way to reach the devices around you.

A cool demo where he split a track into vocals, drums, etc. 

I got a bit of a jolt toward the end, when he talked about us having more data than we think, and I thought could I use the data I have already for my shelf page and use it in different ways, creating a sort of graph view or something and show something in terms of mood, or some new way to look at it.

I realise that the best use of these LLMs is to build tools that are deterministic, which don’t use LLMs for their functioning, or in a very limited fashion. And these tools need to solve your problem, and then you can share it with the world if you want.

## A CEO's workday in a company where Al does most of the work by Jussi Kajala, 3DBear

![CEO in the AI Age](https://storage.sajalchoudhary.net/images/2026/11/vibe-nov-05.jpeg)*CEO in the AI Age*

Main job of the CEO is deciding things now. Agents do everything - creating meetings, sending invites, creating presentations, etc.

A couple of demos including one where teachers build e-courses by talking to agents. The idea is cool, the original dream when thinking about using LLMs to learn, but it looked the same as all these LLM generated things are. 

The headline on last slide was funny to me - agent does the work, a named person owns it.
