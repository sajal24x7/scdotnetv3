I [performed at India Day](https://youtu.be/NtdUtqg6ddY?si=ikv3YCRry6w0ObSQ) on Sunday. I wrote about my experience in [NL67](https://sajalchoudhary.net/nordletter/nl67-performing-at-india-day/). 

I prepared for that dance almost every day for a month and a half. I would drive straight from work to my friend's place, where we would meet with the other members of our group and brainstorm the steps, the music and do some practice. Then do it again.

Now that the performance is done, I find myself with this time I did not have before.

So, what do I do now?
