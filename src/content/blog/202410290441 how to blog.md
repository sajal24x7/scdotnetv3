How should I blog? Or write?

I want to do it everyday. A good format for that might be what Dave does on [scripting news](http://scripting.com). Have a post for each day and write all thoughts there.

Why?  
Because, not all thoughts come with a headline.

And the structure of Ghost is such that each post needs a headline. I wish there were some way to automate it though.
