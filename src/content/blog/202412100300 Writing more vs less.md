# Writing more is better. _Right?_

But I love the elegance of [Steph](https://stephango.com), or [Derek](https://sive.rs) or [Craig](https://craigmod.com). I love the elegance of single word urls: <https://stephango.com/self-guarantee>.

I love the idea of reducing thoughts to their very core. Of editing, of making things simpler.

I love the idea that there might be few things on my website, but they will mean more, somehow.

# Writing lesser, more evergreen notes type of things is better. _Right?_

But I love the daily ritual of opening [Daring Fireball](https://daringfireball.net), or [Seth's blog.](https://seths.blog) How good is that url, btw? <https://seths.blog>.

I love that you would have a reason to open my [website](__GHOST_URL__/) each day.

I love the promise.

I love what it says [about me](__GHOST_URL__/home/). I am a writer, because I write. Every. Single. Day.

I love that it allows me to think.
