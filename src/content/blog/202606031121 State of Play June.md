Sony held a [State of Play](https://www.youtube.com/live/cvh0xXmu0bs?is=JJzew76hMHU8MZej) on 2nd June. There were a bunch of exciting showcases and gameplay reveals. These are the things that excited me.

1. Wolverine. The trailer looked good. I noticed two things. There was a lot of blood, but that blood got off of Wolverine magically. I wondered about the gaming loop, how fun would it be beating this hapless humans? There might be harder enemies to beat, but let’s see.
2. God of War Laufey. I loved the trailer for this one as well. Years ago I had tried to play God of War, but could not continue. I have both of them in my to-be-played list. This just makes me want to play those games and be ready for this.
3. Tomb Raider. Looks fun to play. I have played the three in the remake. I have not played the originals so the story would be new to me.
4. Control Resonant. This looks good too. But nothing new here in this trailer.
5. Until Dawn 2. Until Dawn is another game that I left after starting. This trailer looks similarly moviesque.
6. The Lost World. Don’t know if this one is tied with Jurassic Park/World movies, but looked quite good.
7. Ill. It looked good. A horror game. Not sure if I’ll play it. There are so many Resident Evil games in my TBP pile already.
