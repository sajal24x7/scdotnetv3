Part of [azure governance](/til/governance-overview)

Role based access control / giving something permissions at a certain scope when needed

1. Applies to all items in control plane ([management groups](/til/management-groups),[resource groups](/til/resource-groups), [resources](/til/azure-resources))
2. can apply to some storage roles in data plane
3. Inherited

- 500 per [management group](/til/management-groups)
- 5000 per tenant for [custom roles](/til/azure-rbac-custom-roles)
