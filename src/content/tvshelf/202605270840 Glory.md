I had thought this would be a limited series, but no, they ended it in a cliffhanger.

I liked it. The story follows two brothers on a quest for vengeance, as they find out who killed their sister.

I liked the premise and the setting. The characters felt believable, though the number of times the characters were in peril and somehow managed to escape was a bit unbelievable.

I caught the lead doing the Netflix wants shows/movies to explain what is going on, a few times. So that was funny.
